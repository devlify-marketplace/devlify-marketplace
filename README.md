## About

I’m **Sadiq Abdullahi**, a cybersecurity professional and full-stack developer, and the founder of **[Devlify](https://devlify.com.ng)**. I build secure web and mobile applications, business platforms, AI/ML solutions, and developer tools—turning practical problems into dependable digital products.

My work spans product development, application security, APIs, databases, automation, and deployment. I’m especially interested in software that is useful in the real world, safe to operate, and maintainable as it grows.
